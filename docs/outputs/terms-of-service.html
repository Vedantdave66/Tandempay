2. Description of service

TandemPay is an expense-splitting and debt-settlement coordination tool designed for Canadian users. The Service allows you to:

  • Create groups and track shared household expenses.
  • Calculate balances and simplify debts among group members.
  • Record and coordinate Interac e-Transfer settlements between users.
  • Process card payments via Stripe as a backup settlement method.

Important: TandemPay is an expense-tracking and coordination tool. We do not hold, transfer, or custody funds on your behalf. Interac e-Transfers occur directly between users through their own banks. We record whether a settlement has been confirmed, but we are not a money services business, payment processor, or financial institution for Interac transactions.
