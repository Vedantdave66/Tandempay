1. Who we are

TandemPay ("we", "our", "us") is a Canadian software service that helps roommates and household members track shared expenses and settle debts via Interac e-Transfer and Stripe. We operate the website at tandempay.ca and the TandemPay mobile application (collectively, the "Service").

We are subject to Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation.

2. What information we collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password (stored as a bcrypt hash — we never store your plaintext password).
  • Profile customization: optional display nickname, character avatar, and colour preferences.
  • Expense data: descriptions, amounts, dates, and split configurations for expenses you create.
  • Settlement records: amounts, dates, and the payment method you select (Interac or card).
  • Group and member information: group names and the email addresses of people you invite.

Information collected automatically:

  • Log data: IP address, browser/device type, pages visited, and timestamps, collected when you use the Service.
  • Authentication tokens: short-lived JWTs (60-minute expiry) used to keep you signed in.

Information from third-party services:

  • Stripe: If you use card payment, Stripe processes your card details directly. We receive a Stripe customer ID, last-four digits, card brand, and transaction status. We never see or store your full card number or CVV.
  • Interac e-Transfer email parsing (optional): If you forward a bank confirmation email to our inbound address (anything@inbound.tandempay.ca), SendGrid delivers that email to our server. We extract only the transfer amount, sender name, and date to auto-confirm a settlement. We do not store the raw email body. You choose whether to use this feature.
  • SendGrid: We use SendGrid to send transactional emails (password reset, settlement notifications). SendGrid may log delivery metadata.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Process expense tracking, debt calculation, and settlement confirmation.
  • Send you transactional emails you explicitly request (password reset, settlement notifications you opt into).
  • Improve the Service through aggregate, anonymized usage analytics.
  • Respond to your support requests.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

4. How we share your information

We share information only as follows:

Service providers (sub-processors):

  • Supabase — hosted PostgreSQL database. Your account and expense data is stored in Supabase's infrastructure (AWS us-east-1). Supabase is SOC 2 Type II certified.
  • Vercel — application hosting. Your requests are processed on Vercel's edge network.
  • Stripe — payment processing for card settlements and Pro subscriptions. Stripe is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified. Stripe's privacy policy applies to data Stripe processes: stripe.com/privacy.
  • SendGrid (Twilio) — transactional email delivery and inbound email parsing. Twilio's privacy policy: twilio.com/legal/privacy.

Other disclosures:

  • Group members: Members of a group you create or join can see your display name, avatar, expense entries, and settlement status within that group. They cannot see your email address or payment details unless you share them.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of TandemPay or others.
  • Business transfer: If TandemPay is acquired or merges, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored in Canada and the United States (Supabase/AWS us-east-1). By using TandemPay you consent to this cross-border transfer.

We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit.
  • Bcrypt hashing for passwords.
  • Short-lived JWT tokens (60-minute expiry) with server-side revocation on logout.
  • Rate limiting on authentication endpoints.
  • Database access restricted to application service accounts.
No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. We implement commercially reasonable measures but cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile, expense data, and group memberships are permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Anonymized aggregate analytics data (no PII) may be retained indefinitely.
  • We may retain certain records for longer where required by law (e.g., financial transaction logs for tax purposes).

7. Your rights

Under PIPEDA you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Withdraw consent to our collection or use of your information (subject to legal or contractual restrictions), which may require us to close your account.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@tandempay.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email or in-app notification. Your continued use of the Service after any change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
